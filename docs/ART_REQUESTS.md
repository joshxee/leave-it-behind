- Woven tape that feeds from the engineer's roll and unfolds onto the wall; persistent layered repairs.
- Thin full-height walls pushed toward the outside of each room's grid, with matching sliding doors. The foreground retains its full 22 px face; the earlier low cutaway direction is superseded.

The latest character hold/use/walk animations, ship map, collision cores, door behavior, menus and settings are retained.
