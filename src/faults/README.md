# faults

What breaks, where, and how long until it is fatal. A fault is an entity with a `Fault { site, clock, elapsed, repair }`; spawning `fault_bundle(site, clock)` starts it, and each kind reacts to `Added<Fault>`. Clocks tick in FixedUpdate/Simulate; `resolve_faults` (FixedUpdate/Resolve) despawns repaired faults (`FaultFixed`) and reports fatal ones (`FaultFailed`, which `level` turns into a loss). Every fix takes about 3 s.

- **Vitals (`vitals.rs`):** breaches and loose bolts share a pool per kind, `Vitals { oxygen, heat }` (each a `Pool { spent, drain }`, `spent` 0 fine to 1 fatal). A fault's `clock` is its drain: alone and untouched it spends a full pool in `clock` seconds; two at once drain twice as fast; the drain falls with the repair (`Fault::drain`, `(1 - repair) / clock`: each tight bolt, each second of tape). With nothing draining it a pool recovers from empty to full in `RECOVER_SECS` (30 s). A spent pool loses the flight, blamed on the longest-running fault of its kind. Trajectory drift has no pool: it fails on its own clock. `Vitals::urgency` and `Vitals::time_left` are the one way to ask how close any fault is to failing (the alarm, diagnostics, engine glow and HUD all do). Reset each run (`RunSet::Spawn`), stepped by `drain_vitals` after `tick_fault_clocks`.

| Kind | Sites (`sites.rs`) | Fix | Unfixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LooseBolts` (`bolts.rs`) | 4 engine panels × 3 bolts (the long faces of the engine blocks) | stand, click each loose bolt with the snapped wrench (0.9 s turn each; less with the faster-wrench upgrade) | heats the engine (`Vitals::heat`) until it overheats |
| `HullBreach` (`breach.rs`) | 6 airlock, 3 main hull, 2 cockpit and 2 engine room wall points, equally likely | stand and hold tape on the hole for `SEAL_SECS` (3 s; less with wider tape) | drains the oxygen (`Vitals::oxygen`) until it runs out |
| `TrajectoryDrift` (`drift.rs`) | the nav helm | E at the pilot seat locks in; WASD nudges the drifting marker into the centre band and holds it `HOLD_SECS` (3 s). Progress builds only at the helm and drains otherwise, so a marker that wanders into the band by itself fixes nothing | asteroid collision |

- **Sites (`sites.rs`):** positions come from the ship map: breach points are the marks `ship::map::BREACH_MARKS` on hull walls (the cockpit's and engine room's side walls too, so a breach is often near the other faults), bolt panels the long faces of the engine blocks `'P'` / `'S'` (inner faces look toward the spine), the helm the cockpit's pilot seat. Names are ship-relative (port/starboard, fore/aft) so they hold whichever way the ship points; `label()` (the diagnostic list) is worked out from the layout ("left wall, bottom", "left engine, spine side", "hatch seal, left").
- **Types:** `FaultKind` (names, failure text, `pooled`, default clock range within `CLOCK_LIMITS` 45–90 s), `Vitals`, `Pool`, `Site` (kind, room, position, normal, wall cell, bolt positions), `Fault`, `FaultFixed`, `FaultFailed`, `Bolt`, `Nav` (marker, drift heading, helm engaged).
- **In-room cues (warm colours are for these only):** loose bolts expose their threads and rattle; their nine-frame screw animation retracts into a fixed socket as the wrench turns, the engine block with the loose panel glows with the engine's heat, a breach draws a torn hole on the raised wall face venting a pulsing air ring, the nav marker turns red and the display frame blinks (held red when alarm flashing is off). None of this is visible from another room. Applied woven tape remains as the patch for the rest of the run. All of it freezes while paused.
- **Systems:** `tick_fault_clocks`, `drain_vitals`, `resolve_faults`; bolts: `spawn_bolts`, `tighten_all`, `loosen_bolts`, `tighten_bolts`, `draw_bolts`, `heat_engines`; breach: `open_breaches` (raised tear and air cue), `seal_breaches`, `draw_breaches`; drift: `spawn_helm`, `use_helm` (FixedUpdate/Act), `start_drift` (drift heading from `GameRng`), `steer`, `draw_nav`.
- **Tests:** unit tests in each file; integration tests in `tests/integration/faults.rs`, `tests/integration/tools.rs` and `tests/integration/vitals.rs`; e2e in `e2e/specs/faults.spec.ts` and `e2e/specs/vitals.spec.ts`.
- **Scenarios:** `bolts`, `breach`, `drift`, `scramble`, `breach_critical` (2 s of air left), `second_breach` (a breach on 40% air), `tape_low`.

The nav display is a CRT radar with a rotating sweep, a ship cursor, a square target and hold bar. The ship and square share the exact normalized gameplay mapping; near-band amber and accessibility flash settings are preserved.
